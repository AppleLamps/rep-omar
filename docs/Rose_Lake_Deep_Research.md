## Thread 3: SEC ADV / Form D / Reg D — Rose Lake entity searches

**Status:** Rose Lake Inc. is invisible to the SEC across every name variant and principal name search I ran.

**Searches executed:**
- Full-text EDGAR search: "Rose Lake Inc", "Rose Lake Capital", "Rose Lake LLC", "Rose Lake Partners", "Rose Lake Africa", "Rose Lake Advisors", "Rose Lake Holdings" — zero direct hits
- IAPD adviser search on "Rose Lake" — zero adviser registrations
- Form D private-offering filings — zero
- Principal name searches on Tim Mynett, Henry R. Hailer, Alex Hoffman, Justin Pratt — no Rose Lake-tied filings
- Investment adviser state registration lookups (DC, MN, VA) — zero

**Interpretation:**
Rose Lake Inc. operates as a private advisory firm below SEC registration thresholds, or it is structured deliberately to avoid Investment Advisers Act registration. Three plausible structures:
1. A family office or single-client advisory shell that is exempt under IAA Section 203(b).
2. A consulting firm providing strategic advisory services that does not manage client funds (no securities registration required).
3. An entity that has a management agreement with an affiliate fund vehicle registered separately (and not located because the affiliated vehicle may use a distinct name).

**Why this matters:**
A capital firm with partners who publicly describe themselves as investors in African mining, telecom, and US climate/water infrastructure would normally leave SEC traces if managing third-party capital. The absence of any filing means either: Rose Lake manages only internal principal capital (no third-party LPs), or third-party capital is routed through unnamed fund vehicles that have not been identified.

**Confidence:** corroborated (SEC EDGAR is the authoritative source; absence is dispositive for that database).

**What would disprove:** any Form D, Schedule 13G, or ADV filing surfacing under an affiliated-fund name. Also worth re-running on entity names like "Rose Lake Partners I LP" or similar fund-suffix variants.

**Hand-off:** state securities regulators in DC, MN, and VA; Delaware Secretary of State entity search for Rose Lake fund vehicles.

## Thread 4: DFC / EXIM / USAID / MCC — Pham-adjacent African projects

**Status:** Three major federal-finance transactions line up chronologically with Pham's State Department envoy roles AND his concurrent Rose Lake advisory role. All three involve portfolio companies or sectors Pham was positioned to influence.

**Finding A: Africell — DFC $100M loan**
- DFC approved a $100M loan facility for Africell (African mobile operator) during the period J. Peter Pham served as Special Envoy for the Great Lakes Region of Africa (2020-2021).
- DFC Public Information Summary confirmed transaction, loan tenor, and use-of-proceeds (mobile network expansion in Angola, DRC, Gambia, Sierra Leone).
- Pham's envoy portfolio included DRC, placing him in a position to influence State Department posture toward Africell's DRC rollout during the same window.

**Finding B: Rainbow Rare Earths / TechMet — DFC $50M equity (2021)**
- DFC invested $50M in equity into TechMet, a critical minerals holding company with interests in Rainbow Rare Earths (Burundi, South Africa).
- Pham is listed in Rose Lake advisor bios (Wayback 2024) as a strategic adviser. His concurrent federal role was Special Envoy to the Sahel (transitioning out in early 2021).
- The TechMet/Rainbow rare-earth positioning aligns with a Pham-authored 2020 Atlantic Council paper on critical minerals and African supply chains.

**Finding C: HPX / Ivanhoe Atlantic — MIGA $123.3M political-risk insurance**
- MIGA (World Bank Group) approved $123.3M in political risk insurance for HPX (High Power Exploration, now renamed Ivanhoe Atlantic) for the Nimba iron ore project in Guinea.
- Pham's Rose Lake bio describes his HPX role as "Strategic Advisor." His actual role is Executive Chairman & Interim CEO of the renamed entity Ivanhoe Atlantic (confirmed via Ivanhoe Atlantic press release and LinkedIn).
- MIGA approval came during Pham's Sahel envoy tenure or immediately after.

**USAID / MCC cross-check:**
No direct USAID contract or MCC compact line item naming Rose Lake or Pham was located. However, USAID Power Africa portfolio items in DRC and Angola (where Africell operates) would be worth a targeted FOIA for award-recipient lists covering 2020-2022.

**EXIM cross-check:**
No direct EXIM financing to Rose Lake-orbit entities located. EXIM financing to Africell was not located (Africell is primarily DFC-financed on the US side).

**Pattern:** three high-value federal-finance approvals ($273.3M combined) to Pham-linked African entities during Pham's envoy/advisor tenure. Each one independently defensible on the merits. The pattern together raises serial-conflict questions.

**Confidence:** corroborated on all three transactions (DFC PIS documents, MIGA approval summaries, Ivanhoe Atlantic corporate disclosures).

**What would disprove:** recusal documentation showing Pham formally stepped back from internal State/USG deliberations on each transaction. No such documentation has been located publicly.

**Hand-off:** House Foreign Affairs Committee minority staff; DFC Inspector General; State Department Office of the Inspector General. FOIA on Pham's State Department recusal and ethics memoranda for 2020-2021.
