### A. Rose Lake entity structure (confirmed)

Three separate Delaware entities exist under the Rose Lake name. All three were formed within a seven-month window in 2022-2023.

**Sequencing interpretation:** Holdings LLC was formed first (June 30, 2022), suggesting a parent structure. Capital LLC followed five weeks later (August 5, 2022) as the operating vehicle. Rose Lake Inc. (January 19, 2023) was formed as a Delaware public-benefit corporation, per Hailer's statement to Minnesota Reformer. The UNASSIGNED AGENT flag on Rose Lake Inc. is atypical for a firm claiming $30M+ valuation on a congressional spouse disclosure.

**Beneficial ownership:** Delaware LLCs do not require public disclosure of members, managers, or beneficial owners. The publicly reachable disclosure is limited to registered agent (VCORP Services / Unassigned). Beneficial ownership details are reachable only via federal FinCEN Corporate Transparency Act filings (CTA reporting is not publicly searchable at the entity level) or via subpoena through House Oversight or a court of competent jurisdiction.

- Rose Lake Inc. had $10 in its bank account
- Under oath: "Does Rose Lake have assets under management?" Hailer: "Under a legal term of art, I think the answer is no, yeah"
- Hailer agreed firm's combined value was under $1 million, and said he "couldn't tell you" when asked if it was less than $500,000
- This is directly contradictory to Rep. Omar's 2024 financial disclosure showing Mynett's Rose Lake Capital stake valued at $5M-$25M

### B. DC entity-registry finding (significant)

**Rose Lake Capital LLC** appears on the District of Columbia's **Entity Revocation List** under DC file number **C00007470247**. Placement on this list indicates administrative revocation for compliance failures, typically non-filing of required reports or non-payment of required fees. Revocation status is publicly confirmable via the DC DLCP (Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection) entity search.

**Implication:** Rose Lake Capital's DC qualification to do business is compromised. If the firm is still holding itself out as a DC-based investment firm, this is a regulatory violation distinct from the SEC-registration question.

### E. Why SEC is blind to Rose Lake (resolved)

Rose Lake Capital's absence from SEC EDGAR, IAPD, and state investment adviser registrations (documented in Thread 3 of `Rose_Lake_Deep_Research.md`) aligns with three exemptions Hailer is relying on:

1. **Venture-capital exemption** (Investment Advisers Act § 203(l)) — VC firms are exempt from SEC registration. Rose Lake publicly positions itself as VC.
2. **Family-office exemption** — applies when only qualified "family clients" are advised.
3. **Private-fund adviser exemption** (for advisers with < $150M in private-fund AUM and no other clients) — with Hailer's own testimony that Rose Lake had under $1M, this is the simplest fit.

**Tension with public marketing:** Rose Lake's website has historically claimed advisors with "previous experience with assets under management worth up to $60 billion" and "venture capital management" services. If Rose Lake is actually VC, the $5M-$25M stake valuation on Omar's 2024 disclosure implies outside-investor capital deployed during 2024, which would trigger Form D and/or other exemption-qualifying filings absent elsewhere in SEC databases.

**Current House Oversight posture:** Chairman Comer's February 5, 2026 letter to Mynett demands "all filings, documents, and communications between [eStCru / Rose Lake] and the Securities and Exchange Commission and any other federal and/or state regulatory bodies, including but not limited to any Form(s) ADV, PF, 13F, D, N-1A, N-2, N-CEN, and/or N-PORT." The letter's phrasing presumes such filings may exist or should exist. Response deadline was February 19, 2026. No public indication that Mynett has complied as of April 2026.

### F. What would disprove the structural read

- Any Form D filed by a Rose Lake affiliate naming outside LPs would reframe the firm as a VC-exempt adviser with actual third-party capital
- Any SEC comment letter or investigation docket involving Rose Lake would indicate regulators are already probing
- Recovery of the DC revocation (cure filing + fee payment) would indicate the firm is still being actively operated

Omar's 2024 disclosure showing Mynett's stake at $5M-$25M. **House Oversight is already moving.** Chairman Comer's February 5, 2026 letter demanding SEC-correspondence production by February 19, 2026 means a committee-level investigation is active. Any FOIA or subpoena return that comes out of that process will likely produce material ahead of any private investigation.

**The FOIAs above are additive, not primary.** They exist to document the federal-ethics footprint (Pham) and to settle the "represented the United States" claim (Mynett) using mechanisms the congressional committee would not prioritize.
