# Ivanhoe Atlantic & Rep. Omar / Mynett — Supporting Data Update

**Prepared:** April 18, 2026
**Scope:** Supplement to "Rose Lake Capital LLC Investigative Report" (Dec 27, 2025). Focuses on new corroborating evidence for (a) Ivanhoe Atlantic financials, structure, and geopolitical positioning and (b) Rep. Ilhan Omar / Timothy Mynett disclosure data and the widening federal probe.

---

## Executive Summary

Since the original December 2025 report, three material developments have strengthened and, in one area, complicated its findings.

First, the Ivanhoe Atlantic story has moved from "pre-IPO rumor" to documented, ratified commercial fact. The Concession and Access Agreement with Liberia was ratified by both houses of Liberia's legislature in December 2025, the Environmental and Social Impact Assessment was accepted by Liberia's EPA, and the company committed to approximately $2.0 billion in payments to Liberia over a 25-year concession life. The Kon Kweni resource has been formally sized at 751.9 million tonnes Direct Ship Ore, including 209 million tonnes of high-grade ore at 67.8% Fe, and the company disclosed expected lifecycle revenue to the Government of Guinea of US$16.7 billion.

Second, the ASX float was shelved and then formally scrapped after Rep. John Moolenaar (Chair, House Select Committee on the CCP) sent a December 10, 2025 letter to Secretary Rubio challenging Ivanhoe Atlantic's alleged CCP ties. Founder Robert Friedland pivoted hard into Trump's orbit… appearing behind the President on February 2, 2026 at the Oval Office launch of Project Vault (a $12B critical-minerals stockpile, $10B EXIM loan + $1.67B private). The company is now targeting a US (New York) listing in 2026.

Third, Rep. Omar voluntarily filed an amended disclosure in April 2026 that walks the original $6M–$30M range back to $18,004–$95,000. The amendment relies on a 2025 email from Mynett's accountant valuing Rose Lake Capital at $7.9M and eStCru at $1.5M… with Mynett owning "roughly one-third" of each. This revision resolves the arithmetic question (what was reported) without resolving the substantive question (what the assets are actually worth, and who put money in). House Oversight Chair James Comer's Feb 5, 2026 letter demanded audited financials, SEC filings, and travel records to the UAE, Somalia, and Kenya by Feb 19, 2026. President Trump publicly said DOJ is examining Omar's finances.

Bottom line: the original report's thesis holds. Ivanhoe Atlantic is real, its valuation catalysts have now materialized in ratified form, and the core conflict-of-interest concern around Mynett's private equity exposure to a U.S.-policy-sensitive West African mining project remains intact. The Omar "$30M" headline has been softened by the amendment, but the underlying forensic questions (source of capital, influence vectors, litigation) have intensified, not dissipated.

## 1. Ivanhoe Atlantic — Newly Confirmed Financial and Structural Data

### 1.1 Corrected ownership structure (Kon Kweni project)

The original report stated the project is partly owned by the Government of Guinea. Recent filings give the precise split, which differs from some earlier reporting. [confirmed across sources]

Kon Kweni is held 85% by Société des Mines de Fer de Guinée (SMFG), a subsidiary of Ivanhoe Atlantic, 10% by the Government of Guinea, and 5% by Mifergui (a state-linked Guinean mining entity). Ivanhoe Atlantic's own Guinea page confirms the split, as does Energy Capital Power's reporting on the ESIA approval. Some media (Mining.com, Business Insider Africa) summarize this as "85% Ivanhoe Atlantic / 15% Government of Guinea," collapsing Mifergui into the state stake… that rounding is imprecise but directionally correct.

Ivanhoe Atlantic itself is majority-owned by I-Pulse Inc., founded and chaired by Robert Friedland. Dr. J. Peter Pham serves as executive chairman of Ivanhoe Atlantic (upgraded description from "chairman"); the CEO is Bronwyn Barnes.

### 1.2 Resource, production, and capex figures

- Resource: 751.9 million tonnes total Direct Ship Ore (DSO), including 209 million tonnes of high-grade ore at 67.8% Fe. [confirmed across sources: Ivanhoe Atlantic, Mining.com, Energy Capital Power]
- Grade: Phase 1 ore grading ~66.5% Fe on average… among the highest globally and suitable for Direct Reduced Iron ("green steel") feedstock.
- Phase 1: 2–5 million tonnes per annum (Mtpa), targeting first shipments in 2027.
- Phase 2: Ramp to 25–30 Mtpa, requiring ~$850 million additional investment in export infrastructure.
- Phase 3: Establishment of the "Lola Processing Hub" in Guinea's Lola district.
- Construction start: Q1 2026, per Liberia's International Railway Journal coverage.
- Location advantage: 16 km from Liberia border, 46 km from the northern end of the Yekepa-Buchanan railway.

### 1.3 The Concession and Access Agreement — ratified and priced

The rail corridor deal, the hinge of the entire valuation thesis in the original report, was ratified by Liberia's House of Representatives in mid-December 2025 and by the Senate on December 18, 2025. President Boakai's multi-user rail policy framed the agreement as an open-access reform, breaking ArcelorMittal's prior monopoly on the Yekepa-Buchanan line. [confirmed across sources]

Key financial terms now public:
- Total concession value: ~$1.8 billion, 25-year term, signed July 5, 2025.
- Ivanhoe Atlantic payments to Liberia: ~$1.4 billion in rail user fees + ~$600 million in taxes and other charges = ~$2.0B over concession life.
- Expected lifecycle revenue to Government of Guinea: US$16.7 billion via royalties, taxes, and development fund contributions.
- Rail length: 243 km from Kon Kweni (via Yekepa) to port of Buchanan.

On January 7, 2026, Liberia's EPA accepted Ivanhoe Atlantic's Phase 1 ESIA (prepared by Earthtime Group). That removed the last major Liberian-side regulatory hurdle; parallel ESIA approvals in Guinea were still "finalizing" as of the most recent reporting.

### 1.4 The ASX IPO — shelved, then scrapped

The original report correctly flagged the planned A$200–300M ASX float. New facts:

- October 2025: Australian Financial Review ("Street Talk") publicly questioned the company's China linkages ahead of the float… calling the miner's denials "doth protest too much."
- December 9–10, 2025: Rep. John Moolenaar (R-MI), Chair of the House Select Committee on the CCP, sent a formal letter to Secretary of State Marco Rubio flagging Ivanhoe Atlantic's "concerning ties" to the CCP via I-Pulse and Friedland's separate Ivanhoe Mines vehicle. [confirmed across sources: Reuters, Mining.com, Select Committee letter]
- Mid-December 2025: Capital Brief reported the ASX float was shelved until at least Q2 2026.
- January 2026: Company publicly confirmed US IPO pivot (New York), with executive chair Peter Pham telling the Financial Times the Trump administration's mining support "affected our calculus."
- February 2, 2026: Friedland joined Trump at the Oval Office for launch of Project Vault, a $12B strategic critical-minerals stockpile (Ivanhoe Mines, Ivanhoe Electric confirmed representation at the announcement).
- The ASX secondary listing option is described as possible "in the future" but has been formally scrapped as the primary route.

Moolenaar's specific allegations: I-Pulse (the majority shareholder) connects to Ivanhoe Mines, which had "units of" CITIC and Zijin Mining owning nearly 39.5% as of 2020 (the original December report cited ~33%; Reuters and MINING.com report 39.5% — [conflicting accounts] but both within striking range). CITIC Telecom has been placed on FCC "unacceptable threat" lists; Zijin was added to the UFLPA Entity List in 2025 over forced-labor concerns.

Ivanhoe Atlantic's on-record denials: "grossly incorrect and misleading"; CEO Barnes: "None will go to China, and as an American company committed to U.S. national security, we will not export through China's Trans-Guinean Railway." [single source for the direct quote: Business Insider Africa]

### 1.5 Valuation implications for Rose Lake's stake

The pivot from ASX to US listing matters for Rose Lake Capital's "mark-to-market" valuation in two ways:

First, it delays the liquidity event. A New York listing in 2026 is plausible but not certain; mining-sector US IPOs have been rare, and Friedland's alignment with Project Vault is signaling but not underwriting. [inference]

Second, aligning with Trump's critical-minerals agenda potentially lifts the floor valuation. The Lobito Corridor received a $553 million DFC loan in December 2025 as an anti-China critical-minerals play; Kon Kweni fits the same playbook in West Africa. If US government backing (EXIM, DFC, or Project Vault offtake) materializes, the Ivanhoe Atlantic valuation could exceed the A$300M IPO benchmark. [inference]

The forensic question for Rose Lake remains: whether the ~$25M upper-range valuation on Omar's 2024 disclosure reflected a real pre-IPO round (with new outside money) or an internal markup tied to the rail deal signing. The amended April 2026 filing (see §2.2) now implies the latter.

## 2. Omar / Mynett — Disclosure Dispute, Amendment, and the Federal Probe

### 2.1 Original 2024 filing recap (for comparison)

- Rose Lake Capital: $1-$1,000 (2023) → $5,000,001–$25,000,000 (2024)
- eStCru LLC: $15,001–$50,000 (2023) → $1,000,001–$5,000,000 (2024)
- Reported combined range: $6,000,001–$30,000,000
- Income from Rose Lake Capital in 2024: None reported

### 2.2 The amended disclosure (April 2026) — new data point

On or around April 17–18, 2026, the New York Post and Wall Street Journal reported that Omar had voluntarily filed an amended disclosure substantially revising the numbers downward. [confirmed: NY Post, WSJ via NY Post]

Key figures from the amendment:
- Amended combined asset range: $18,004–$95,000 (a ~99.7% reduction from the upper bound of the original filing)
- Liability disclosure updated: $15,001–$50,000 in student loan debt, similar range in credit card debt
- Basis for the amendment: a 2025 email from Mynett to his accountant valuing Rose Lake Capital at $7.9 million and eStCru at $1.5 million on an enterprise basis, with Mynett owning "roughly one-third" of each.
- Omar spokesperson Jacklyn Rogers: "The amended disclosure confirms what we've said all along: The congresswoman is not a millionaire… The congresswoman amended her disclosures voluntarily as soon as the discrepancy was identified."

Math check [inference]: if Rose Lake enterprise value = $7.9M and Mynett holds ~33%, his stake ≈ $2.6M; if eStCru enterprise value = $1.5M and Mynett holds ~33%, his stake ≈ $495K. Combined ≈ $3.1M. That does not reconcile with a reported range of $18,004–$95,000 unless the amendment is netting against liabilities or valuing Mynett's interest using a distressed/no-market-value methodology. The Fox News summary notes the businesses were listed as "having no net value once liabilities were factored in." That is a defensible accounting position for privately held, illiquid, pre-revenue ventures but is difficult to reconcile with the enterprise valuations Mynett himself told his accountant. [conflicting accounts — the discrepancy is structural, not clerical]

### 2.3 Court-record liquidity data — independent corroboration

Court filings in the South Dakota Badlands cannabis litigation (discovery responses from Hailer, February 2024) put the operating cash position of the principal entities in stark terms. [confirmed: Minnesota Reformer via Investigative Economics]

- eSt Ventures: $0.05 in bank account
- Rose Lake Capital: $42.44
- Rose Lake Inc.: $10.00
- eStCru: $650.00
- Hailer's personal checking: $3.05

These figures are consistent with the original report's "solvency anomaly" thesis. A firm with $42.44 in the bank in early 2024 being revalued to $5M–$25M by year-end 2024 requires either (a) a major new equity injection from unnamed investors or (b) a paper markup tied to an unrealized event (e.g., Ivanhoe Atlantic's pre-IPO valuation). The amended April 2026 filing implicitly concedes option (b).

### 2.4 Rose Lake spokesperson disclosure (January 2026)

In first public comments, a Rose Lake spokesperson told the New York Post [single source, but directly quoted]:

- Mynett's partnership interest: "less than one-third of the company"
- Mynett's 2024 income from all business ventures: less than $20,000
- Mynett's 2025 income expected to be "similar numbers"
- Total company valuation: "a seven-figure number"
- eStCru: "dozens of registered trademarks for brands"

The "seven-figure" framing is inconsistent with the Mynett-to-accountant $7.9M valuation for Rose Lake alone (which could be low eight figures combined with eStCru). [conflicting accounts within subject's own camp]

### 2.5 The House Oversight probe — scope and document demands

Chairman James Comer (R-KY) transmitted a formal letter to Mynett on February 5, 2026, demanding production by February 19, 2026 of:

1. Audited financial statements for all fiscal years from 2020 forward for both eStCru LLC and Rose Lake Capital LLC.
2. All filings and communications between eStCru and the SEC or any other federal/state regulator, "including but not limited to any Form(s) ADV, PF, 13F, D, N-1A, N-2, N-CEN, and/or N-PORT." (The enumeration is notable… these are the SEC forms used by investment advisers, private funds, and mutual-fund complexes. If Rose Lake has never filed any of these despite claiming $60B AUM on its website, that is itself a federal-regulatory question.)
3. All equivalent filings and communications for Rose Lake Capital.
4. All documents and communications related to travel by anyone affiliated with Rose Lake Capital or eStCru to the United Arab Emirates, Somalia, or Kenya — including dates, travelers, and stated purpose.

The UAE / Somalia / Kenya travel-records demand is the most newsworthy element and was not in the original report. It signals the Committee is actively probing whether investment capital in Rose Lake traces back to East African or Gulf sources with ties to Rep. Omar's foreign-policy portfolio. This dovetails with the Minnesota "Feeding Our Future" fraud investigation Comer is also running, and with long-standing allegations regarding Somali-American political networks.

### 2.6 Federal law-enforcement angle

Two new data points on federal interest, both [single source, law-enforcement source]:
- Trump publicly said in late 2025 / early 2026 that the DOJ is examining Omar's finances.
- Per the NY Post's January 17, 2026 exclusive, the FBI was briefed during the Biden administration on suspicions about Mynett's network of companies. Associates reportedly brought information to federal investigators in 2024.

### 2.7 Political context — the "foreign fraud investigation" frame

Comer's letter explicitly ties the Rose Lake probe to the Minnesota "widespread fraud in social services programs" investigation. The "MEALS Act" / "Feeding Our Future" controversy remains the proximate political vehicle for applying pressure. Omar's office has characterized the Comer probe as a "political stunt" and "smear campaign." This characterization is, on its face, politically motivated — but the underlying forensic anomalies (the valuation jump, the bank-account data, the litigation pattern, and the opaque cap table) are independent of partisan framing and remain unexplained on the record.

## 4. Contested / Uncertain Terrain

**What is the real valuation of Rose Lake Capital?** The evidence points three different directions: Mynett's own accountant email ($7.9M), the original 2024 disclosure upper range ($25M), and the amended 2026 filing (effectively near-zero net value). [conflicting accounts] A pre-IPO Ivanhoe Atlantic stake marked to an unrealized A$300M float valuation would plausibly land somewhere in the $5M–$25M range if Rose Lake holds early-stage equity or advisory shares; a mark to the scrapped float valuation is now stale. No independent SEC-registered valuation exists.

**What is the actual cap table at Rose Lake?** Despite Comer's document demand, the investor list and LP structure remain non-public. The UAE/Somalia/Kenya travel demand signals Comer suspects foreign-investor involvement, but no evidence has surfaced publicly. [unverified]

**Did Mynett/Hailer pay for Ivanhoe Atlantic equity in cash, or receive it as compensation for political access?** The original report's inference that Pham (as Ivanhoe Atlantic executive chair and former U.S. Special Envoy) brought the opportunity to Rose Lake in exchange for influence remains the most parsimonious explanation but is [inference] not documented.

**How much CCP / China exposure does Ivanhoe Atlantic actually carry?** Moolenaar's argument relies on the corporate-cousin relationship (Ivanhoe Atlantic / Ivanhoe Mines / I-Pulse / Friedland) and the CITIC + Zijin minority stakes in Ivanhoe Mines. Ivanhoe Atlantic's rebuttal — that it is a separate legal entity with no direct Chinese ownership and that no ore will move through Chinese-controlled logistics — is factually accurate at the legal-entity level. Whether "sister company" influence is material is a judgment call that the Trump administration (via Project Vault alignment) has answered in Ivanhoe Atlantic's favor… for now. [conflicting accounts, resolution pending]

**Is the amended Omar disclosure accurate?** The arithmetic in the amendment (near-zero net value) does not reconcile cleanly with Mynett's own accountant's enterprise valuations. Both numbers cannot be simultaneously correct unless the "net value after liabilities" framing is doing heavy lifting. Expect further sparring from Oversight. [unresolved]

## 5. Bottom-Line Assessment

The original December 27, 2025 Rose Lake report has been substantially corroborated by events in the four months since. Ivanhoe Atlantic is a real, increasingly valuable, and geopolitically entangled asset whose value has been unlocked by a diplomatic breakthrough (the Liberian rail concession) that plausibly benefited from political facilitation — the central thesis of the original report.

The Omar/Mynett story has moved from "suspicious disclosure" to "active federal probe with document demands." The April 2026 amended disclosure does clean up the $30M headline, but it does so by walking back the original filing rather than by explaining the underlying capital flows. The court-record bank balances ($42.44 in Rose Lake's account in early 2024) remain the most damaging single data point for any "legitimate operating business" narrative.

Two forensic priorities for the next cycle of investigation: (1) whether the Comer document production (due Feb 19, 2026) actually materialized and what it revealed about the cap table; (2) the valuation at which Ivanhoe Atlantic ultimately lists in New York, which will test whether the Rose Lake paper markup was anchored to economic reality or to political access.

Confidence level: High on Ivanhoe Atlantic facts (multi-source, primary documents, ratified legislation). Medium on Omar/Mynett financial amendment (derives from a single WSJ document review cycle; expect follow-on reporting). Low on the cap-table question (opaque by design, no primary source has surfaced publicly).
