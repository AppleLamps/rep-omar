### 1.1 Hailer's sworn bankruptcy testimony (single most damaging artifact)

**The site's "Paper Tiger" section makes the markup-vs-cash case but doesn't have the kill quote.**

In the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware on November 21, 2024, Will Hailer testified under oath that:
- Rose Lake Capital had **$42.44 in its bank account in 2022**
- Rose Lake Inc. had **$10 in its bank account**
- Combined firm value was **under $1 million**, and he "couldn't tell you" if it was less than $500,000
- Asked "Does Rose Lake have assets under management?" Hailer responded: "Under a legal term of art, I think the answer is no, yeah"

**Why it matters:** This is sworn testimony from one co-founder directly contradicting the $5M-$25M figure on the other co-founder's spouse's congressional disclosure. There is no rhetorical move that defangs this contradiction. Quote it verbatim from the court transcript.

**Recommended placement:** New callout box in the "Paper Tiger" section, adjacent to the disclosure table. Title it something like "Sworn Testimony vs. Disclosure Filing."

**Source:** NY Post Feb 7, 2026 reporting (https://nypost.com/2026/02/07/us-news/ilhan-omar-says-her-hubbys-firm-was-worth-up-to-25-million-but-partner-told-court-it-had-almost-no-capital/) and the underlying Voizzit Chapter 11 docket in D. Del. (the site already references this docket but doesn't pull the substance).

### 1.2 House Oversight Committee letter (Feb 5, 2026, Chairman Comer)

**Site doesn't currently reference this. It's a major escalation.**

Chairman James Comer of the House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform sent a formal letter to Tim Mynett on February 5, 2026, opening an investigation tied to the Minnesota Feeding Our Future fraud scheme. The letter demands by February 19, 2026:

- Audited financial statements for eStCru and Rose Lake Capital from 2020 to present
- All filings and communications between either firm and the SEC and any other federal/state regulators, including specifically Form ADV, PF, 13F, D, N-1A, N-2, N-CEN, N-PORT
- Source-of-funds documentation for the valuation jump from up to $51,000 (2023) to up to $30M (2024)

**Why it matters:** A sitting committee chairman with subpoena authority has formally opened an investigation explicitly framed around the same valuation question your site raises. This is institutional validation of the inquiry.

**Recommended placement:** Either a new section ("Congressional Oversight") between "Conflicts" and "China Ties," or a callout box at the top of the Overview banner.

**Source:** https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Letter-to-Timothy-Mynett-.pdf and press release at https://oversight.house.gov/release/comer-requests-financial-records-from-companies-linked-to-rep-ilhan-omars-husband/

- **No claims about Omar's personal life, marriage history, or immigration history.** These are well-trodden, polarizing, and don't strengthen the financial case. The site is wisely focused on the money trail.
