# Rose Lake Capital — Wayback Recovery of Scrubbed Advisor Roster

**Subject:** Rose Lake Inc. (roselakecap.com), the private capital firm co-founded by Tim Mynett, husband of Rep. Ilhan Omar (D-MN-05).

**Status:** Firm scrubbed its public Team page after press scrutiny. This document reconstructs the full advisor roster, verbatim claimed credentials, and corporate self-description from Wayback Machine archives.

**Recovery method:** Wayback CDX API queries against `roselakecap.com`, followed by individual snapshot fetches of each archived bio URL. Snapshots dated March 2023 through August 2024.

**Confidence:** Every quoted passage is verbatim from archived HTML at `web.archive.org`. Snapshot dates and URL paths listed in §13 (Recovery Status).

## 1. Why this matters

Rose Lake Inc. publicly removed its advisor and team pages following press coverage of the Mynett/Omar nexus. The scrubbed roster, recovered in full below, contains nine publicly-listed advisors plus three additional individuals (Peterson, Healy, Knuth) who held bio pages but were not on the most-recent /team index. Two of those three were quietly removed before the broader scrub.

The roster is not a generic capital-firm advisory board. It is a tightly clustered group of (a) Perez-era DNC finance leadership, (b) Minnesota Democratic political figures, (c) former U.S. ambassadors with concurrent foreign-government and African-extractive-industry ties, and (d) labor-bank leadership. Rose Lake's own /about page states it pitches "co-investment alongside… governments" and "the public relations benefits of investing in SRI projects" as selling points.
